## 1. Principles of Automated Financial Narratives

All text, explanations, and summaries generated by the FundFlow Sentinel platform must be **strictly rule-based and derived from computed values only**.

### 1.1 Strictly Prohibited Language & Concepts
The platform enforces automated tests to guarantee that generated narratives **never** contain sensationalist, unhedged, or alarmist financial claims:
* **No Crisis or Solvency Assertions:** Strictly prohibits words such as `"liquidity crisis"`, `"run on the fund"`, `"panic"`, `"insolvency"`, `"insolvent"`, `"fire sale"`, or `"default"`.
* **No Causal Inferences:** The platform measures empirical statistical associations and mathematical sensitivities. It **never** asserts causal drivers such as *"investor panic caused the outflow"* or *"market collapse forced redemptions"*.
* **No Guaranteed Claims:** Forecasts are probabilistic statistical estimates, not certainties. Prohibits terms like `"guaranteed outflow"` or `"certain decline"`.
* **No Holdings-Level Liquidity Overreach:** The platform models category-level gross redemption rates. It has no access to underlying scheme portfolio holdings, cash buffers, bid-ask spreads, secondary market debt liquidity, or settlement queue constraints. The narrative must **never** claim to evaluate holdings liquidation feasibility.

---

## 2. Mandatory Structural Disclosures

Every generated stress scenario narrative must append the four institutional disclaimers:
1. **Illustrative Sensitivity Only:** Scenarios represent linear arithmetic projections applying hypothetical redemption rates to historical closing AUM.
2. **Reference AUM Proxy:** Closing AUM is a historical category-level proxy, not real-time intraday assets or liquid cash reserves.
3. **No Liquidity Portfolio Modeling:** This analysis does not model cash buffers, asset sale settlement delays, market depth, or liquidation price impact.
4. **Not Investment Advice:** Scenario outputs are research metrics for asset liability monitoring and do not predict fund performance or distress.

---

## 3. Transparency on Data Limitations

When data history is inadequate:
* The narrative explicitly states: *"Empirical P10/P90 residual uncertainty bands are currently classified as insufficient_history... statistical interval bounds are honestly withheld rather than fabricated."*
* If baseline forecast is unavailable, the narrative states that baseline is unavailable and avoids deriving speculative incremental outflows.
